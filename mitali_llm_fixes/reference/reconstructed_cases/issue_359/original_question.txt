Solution explanation:
When you call method to add instruction to your circuit, you can specify a label. After circuit execution, the will be saved to dictionary where the key will be that label. If you don't specify a label, will be used by default. And when you simulate your circuit using , the result will be saved, similarly, to dictionary. The key this time also will be . Clearly, this will cause an issue if you add instruction to your circuit then use to execute it. A simple workaround is to call with a label: then retrieve the value using this label:

save_statevector()
