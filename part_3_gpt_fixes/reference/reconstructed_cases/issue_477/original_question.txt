Question:
Title: Is it possible to set quantum volume in IBM Quantum simulator?

A quantum volume serves as a metric of a quantum processor quality. So far, IBM Quantum provides processors with quantum volume up to 128. Freely accessbile processors have QV up to 32. A user using free access can employ fake processors (see details here) to simulate behavior of her/his circuits on processor with QV higher than 32. However what about simulation of processors with QV above 128? Is it possible to somehow set to behave like a processor with given quantum volume?

```
qasm_simulator
```

Original buggy code:
qasm_simulator

Solution explanation:
Bear in mind that if you operate on a subset of $n$ qubits, then your QV is at most $2^n$. Hence the answer below assumes that you'd like to experiment with a simulator that has $n$ qubits but some $QV = 2^m$ where $m < n$. I am not aware of a solution out of the box, but you could: specify noise models and coupling map for your simulator compute such setup's QV iterate on noise parameters until you reach your desired QV. By definition your simulator has QV essentially equal to 2 to the power of number of qubits, so you are essentially trying to force some limitations onto it.
