Solution explanation:
Indeed, your circuit can be reduced to an identity circuit as @KAJ226 nicely explains here. The transpilation process notices that and reduces your gates to idle during circuit depth optimization: You can avoid the optimization by reducing the optimization level to $0$: In your example, you need to modify the call like this:

```
qreg_q = QuantumRegister(1, 'q') creg_c = ClassicalRegister(1, 'c') circuit = QuantumCircuit(qreg_q, creg_c) circuit.u(pi/2, pi/2, pi/2, qreg_q[0]) circuit.x(qreg_q[0]) circuit.y(qreg_q[0]) circuit.x(qreg_q[0]) circuit.y(qreg_q[0]) circuit.u(pi/2, pi/2, pi/2, qreg_q[0]).inverse() circuit.measure(qreg_q[0], creg_c[0]) transpile(circuit, backend=backend).draw('mpl')
```

```
qreg_q = QuantumRegister(1, 'q') creg_c = ClassicalRegister(1, 'c') circuit = QuantumCircuit(qreg_q, creg_c) circuit.u(pi/2, pi/2, pi/2, qreg_q[0]) circuit.x(qreg_q[0]) circuit.y(qreg_q[0]) circuit.x(qreg_q[0]) circuit.y(qreg_q[0]) circuit.u(pi/2, pi/2, pi/2, qreg_q[0]).inverse() circuit.measure(qreg_q[0], creg_c[0]) transpile(circuit, backend=backend).draw('mpl')
```
