Title: Qiskit: bind parameters to sub-circuit

I am trying to implement a Hadamard test on a parametrized sub-circuit $V(\bf{a})$. I create an outer circuit and then append the parametrized $V(\bf{a})$ to it. When I finally contruct the bigger circuit and try to bind the parameters, I get the following error: How do I get overcome this problem?

Solution explanation:
When you append a sub-circuit to a larger circuit, the sub-circuit parameters are added to list of circuit parameters. You can easily check this as follows: So, you can bind them as any parameters in the larger circuit. The issue is that the keys of the parameter dictionary must be the instances not the names[see here]. So, you need to call as follows: Of course to do this the variable needs to be in the scope when you call . The fastest way to do this in your case - although not recommended - is to make it global:

```
operator = subcircuit() print(operator.parameters) test = outer_circuit(operator) print(test.parameters)
```
