Solution explanation:
TL;DR: With : A list of them, via : The QuantumCircuit constructor documentation can be found here: (list() or list() or list(list())) – The registers to be included in the circuit. This means, that the registers , , and are included. Their length is 8, 2, and 4 respectably. However, is a classical register, which does not extend the amount of qubit. So, $8+4=12$.

```
QuantumCircuit.num_qubits
```
