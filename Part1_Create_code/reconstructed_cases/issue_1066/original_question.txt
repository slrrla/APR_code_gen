Solution explanation:
Regarding your first question, consider the following general circuit for what you are trying to accomplish: Here, $|\psi\rangle$ can be an $n$-qubit state, and $V$ is the unitary that prepares a general $1$-qubit superposition state: $$ V|0\rangle = \alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle .$$ To achieve this preparation, the first column of the $V$ matrix must therefore be $[\alpha, \beta]^{\top} .$ The second column of $V$ must be such that $V$ remains unitary. A common choice (and the one used by Qiskit's function) is to have the second column be $[-\beta^*, \alpha^*]^{\top}, $ resulting in: $$ V = \begin{bmatrix}\alpha & -\beta^* \\ \beta & \alpha^* \end{bmatrix} $$ This means that the conjugate transpose of $V$ is: $$ V^{\dagger} = \begin{bmatrix}\alpha^* & \beta^* \\ -\beta & \alpha \end{bmatrix} $$ With this, you can then analyze what happens through the circuit: $$ |0\rangle|\psi\rangle \xrightarrow{V\otimes I} \big(\alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle \big)|\psi\rangle \xrightarrow{CU} \alpha|0\rangle |\psi\rangle + \beta|1\rangle U |\psi\rangle \xrightarrow{V^{\dagger}\otimes I} \alpha \big(\alpha^*|0\rangle - \beta|1\rangle \big) |\psi\rangle + \beta \big(\beta^*|0\rangle + \alpha|1\rangle \big) U |\psi\rangle $$ So, the output of the circuit is: $$\big(|\alpha|^2|0\rangle - \alpha \beta|1\rangle \big) |\psi\rangle + \big(|\beta|^2|0\rangle + \alpha \beta|1\rangle \big) U |\psi\rangle .$$ Usually, you want to make a decision based on what the top qubit's state is, so you can also reorganize the above as: $$|0\rangle \otimes \big(|\alpha|^2 I + |\beta|^2 U\big)|\psi\rangle + |1\rangle \otimes \alpha \beta \big(U - I \big)|\psi\rangle.$$ Regarding your second question, the from Qiskit Aer seems to have issues with some of the blocks used by the function.
