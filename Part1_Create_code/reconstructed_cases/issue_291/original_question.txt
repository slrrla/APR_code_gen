Solution explanation:
In the tutorial, tomography on parallel subsystems assumes that subsystems are independent. From your post, I deduce you are interested in something different, i.e., performing tomography of one state after a single circuit has been executed on several qubits. To this purpose, just modify your code using . In this way, you will obtain the density matrix of $q_1$. Analytically, such a matrix can be obtained as the partial trace with respect to the density matrix of whole output state -- $I/2$, in your example. Indeed, your result will be (about) $I/2$, as expected.
