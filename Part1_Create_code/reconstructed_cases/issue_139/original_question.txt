Solution explanation:
In qiskit, if there are classical bits that are not getting a value through a measurement gate, they will default to . Since you set your circuit up to have 9 classical bits, the will always be bit strings of length 9, even though you are only measuring 1 qubit. To only measure one qubit, and have that be the only value in the resulting bit string, you need to only have one classical bit in your circuit.

'0'
